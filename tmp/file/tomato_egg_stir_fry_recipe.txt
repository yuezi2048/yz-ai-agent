番茄炒蛋 (Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry)

A classic Chinese comfort food that's both delicious and easy to prepare. This simple recipe requires only basic ingredients and takes minutes to cook.

INGREDIENTS:
- 2 ripe tomatoes (about 200g), peeled, cored and cut into bite-sized wedges
- 4 large eggs
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon potato starch (or cornstarch)
- 1 teaspoon soy sauce
- 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
- White pepper (to taste)
- Cilantro or green onions (for garnish)

EQUIPMENT:
- Non-stick frying pan or wok
- Medium glass bowl
- Small glass bowl
- Cooking chopsticks or spatula

INSTRUCTIONS:

1. PREPARE TOMATOES: Add the peeled tomatoes, 2 tablespoons ketchup (optional but recommended for flavor enhancement), potato starch, salt, and white pepper to a bowl and toss together until evenly mixed.

2. BEAT EGGS: Break the eggs into a separate bowl and add soy sauce. Beat gently until just combined - avoid overbeating which makes eggs tough.

3. HEAT PAN: Heat your frying pan over medium-high heat until hot, then add vegetable oil.

4. STIR-FRY TOMATOES: Add the seasoned tomatoes and stir-fry until they soften and release their juices (about 2-3 minutes). The mixture should thicken slightly.

5. ADD EGGS: Pour the beaten eggs over the tomatoes, then immediately reduce heat to low.

6. COOK EGGS: Let eggs cook undisturbed until bottom layer turns opaque. Gently stir curds to surface, letting liquid run underneath. Repeat until eggs are no longer runny but still shiny and moist.

7. FINISH: Quickly scramble mixture and transfer to serving plate. Garnish with cilantro or green onions.

TIPS FOR SUCCESS:
- Peel tomatoes first for better texture (use boiling water method)
- Use fresh, high-quality eggs for best color and flavor
- Don't overcook eggs - they should be creamy and moist, not dry
- Adjust seasoning to taste - add more salt or pepper as needed
- Serve immediately over steamed rice for a complete meal

NUTRITION PER SERVING:
- Calories: 222 kcal
- Protein: 12g
- Carbohydrates: 9g
- Fat: 15g
- Sodium: 863mg

This dish is vegetarian, dairy-free, and ready in under 10 minutes!
